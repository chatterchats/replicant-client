# NPCs

The galaxy isn't just for us players. A handful of NPC replicants are out there too. Some have shops! Track them down to discover interesting trades and location events.

## Riker

Riker is parked at `SOL-3`, where he's working on terraforming Earth for humanity to return one day. Travel to Earth, find him, and scan the planet to kick off the [seasonal story](../story/index.md). He's the entry point to unlock story content.

He also runs a [shop](../../trading/directory/index.md) on the moon called *Riker's Lunar Supplies*. Worth a visit while you're in the system to learn about trading and pick up something unique.

## Bill

Bill keeps his location secret. He moves to a new system every day after checking in on Riker. The directory will show his shop, *The Skunkworks*, but his location is masked.

If you want the challenge of tracking him down, drop an [FTL beacon](../../ftl-beacons/index.md) in the `SOL` system and check its audit log the next day. Bill should show up with his direction vector recorded (yay, maths!) - follow the beacon trail from system to system and you'll eventually catch up to him. His shop has some interesting trades.

## Riker's clones

Riker created three clones to travel with the Exodus Ark as it journeys to distribute humanity across twelve new locations.

### Ledger

Running the logistics and keeping a tight rein on the Ark's travel route. Ledger makes sure everything goes where it's supposed to go.

### Shepherd

In charge of human relations. He actually enjoys the meetings.

### Dash

The scout. Dash handles in-system surveys and any issues that crop up along the way. He runs a [shop](../../trading/directory/index.md) in the Oort cloud of whichever system the Ark is currently in.

## Bill's clones

Bill created two clones to travel to the Delta region to investigate the wormhole.

### Kepler

Handling the theoretical science behind the wormhole. Based in `DELTA`.

### Patch

Experimenting with the wormhole tech. Based in `DELTA`.
