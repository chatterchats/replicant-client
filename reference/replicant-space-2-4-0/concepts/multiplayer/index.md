Core Concepts

# Multiplayer

Replicant Space is a collaborative game. Players can work together to achieve long-term goals as the story progresses over seasons.

Most players will start by focusing on getting their own manufacturing operations up and running. Once you have a foothold, it is time to see what, or who, else is out there.

## Join BobNet

Join the in-game chat system by activating an [FTL relay](../../ftl-relays/index.md) in your system. BobNet gives you a way to talk to other players, follow NPC announcements, and coordinate around [seasonal story](../story/index.md) objectives.

See [BobNet](../bobnet/index.md) for channels, sending messages, webhooks, and catching up on chat history.

## Trade for what you need

Once you find some unique blueprints, establish a [shop](../../trading/configuration/index.md) so other players can obtain any devices missing from their blueprint list. If your sector of the galaxy is short on specific resources, this can be a good way to help share.

## Show who you are

Configure your [public profile](../../api/replicants/configure/index.md) for the [replicant directory](../../api/replicants/directory/index.md) to give each of your replicants their own personality. This makes it easier for other players to understand who they have met and what you are working on.

## Meet your neighbours

When you land in a new system, say hi to the other replicants you see in the [system scan](../../api/replicants/scan/index.md). You can also [scan nearby devices](../../api/replicants/scan/devices/index.md) to browse the other players' infrastructure around you.
